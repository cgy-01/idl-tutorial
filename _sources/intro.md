# About

This book is a tutorial for beginners on the basic syntax and practical applications of the IDL language. IDL is widely used in remote sensing image processing and information extraction. Its integration with the remote sensing software ENVI makes it a powerful tool for remote sensing-related work.

The book is also a record of the undergraduate course "IDL for Remote Sensing Secondary Development" at the School of Remote Sensing & Geomatics Engineering, NUIST, taught by Professor Yongming Xu.The course covers the IDL basics and its applications, including theoretical classes and experimental classes.The textbook for the course is ***《遥感二次开发语言IDL》***, authored by Professor Xu, 2nd Edition.
